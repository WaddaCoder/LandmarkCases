PPC Navigator

Murder (S.299-302 PPC)
Z.A. Bhutto v. State (1979)

Citation: PLD 1979 SC 53

A Prime Minister convicted as an abettor of murder. Established that S.302/34/109 apply to all regardless of political position.

Qandeel Baloch / S.311 Test Case

Citation: State v. Muhammad Waseem (2019/2022)

Application of S.311 (Fasad-fil-arz). Parental forgiveness under Diyat was held legally ineffective in honour killing cases.

Blasphemy (S.295-B / 295-C PPC)
Mumtaz Qadri v. State (2016)

Citation: PLD 2016 SC 17

Rejected vigilante justice; established that enforcement of S.295-C is the state's exclusive domain.

Asia Bibi v. State (2019)

Citation: PLD 2019 SC 64

Recognized the "weaponisation" of blasphemy allegations. Introduced the five red-flag evidentiary framework.

Common Intention (S.34 PPC)
Mahbub Shah v. Emperor (1945)

Citation: AIR 1945 PC 118

The foundational precedent for S.34. Established that common intention requires a pre-arranged plan and acting in concert.

Priyantha Kumara Lynching (2021)

Citation: ATC Sialkot 2021/2022

Modern application of S.34 to mob violence. 88 convictions established that active participation in a mob creates liability for murder.
